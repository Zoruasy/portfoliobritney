VR ACT meditation

Internship project · VR · Unity

VR ACT translates to Acceptance and Commitment Therapy into a physical VR experience for students dealing with stress and anxiety. I made it during my internship: a calm, car ride in which you are invited to sit, look around and reflect, not told what to do.

I built it in Unity with URP, XR Interaction Toolkit and OpenXR: the buttons and their feedback, the radio and audio manager, the fade in around the camera, and a spawn point that waits for tracking before it drops you in. Comfort steered everything. The car never moves, lighting is baked, assets are low poly, and after talking to a psychologist we cut the lobby entirely.

Internship Unity URP XR Interaction Toolkit OpenXR C#

One Last Visit

One-person project · Unity 6

A liminal horror prototype set in an abandoned indoor playground, made on my own from the first scene to the last sound. It's the place you remember, just empty and slightly wrong. No jump scares, only lighting, sound and small events: a dinosaur that slowly turns, balls that wobble as you walk past.

I built it in Unity 6: the player controller, an interaction system for doors, levers, pickups and the flashlight, trigger-based audio and room events, and a checklist. Physics stayed deliberately cheap: wobbling balls instead of a simulation. The project was on Git LFS from the first commit.
